# Related work

Checked 14 November 2026 (America/Indianapolis). Published numbers below are references, not results of this project.

**Ma et al. (2026), 828 Mandarin stand-up sets.** Pauses were gaps between timestamped ASR speech segments; sentence boundaries came from ASR. Their semantic measure was **1 − cosine similarity between adjacent sentence embeddings**, using `text-embedding-3-small`, not next-token surprisal. Within each performance, the highest and lowest distance quintiles defined surprise groups. Across performances, preceding pauses averaged 1.37 versus 1.01 seconds (+360 ms; t(1638)=6.15). In high-performing sets the contrast was 1.61−1.14 seconds (+470 ms), versus 1.07−0.84 (+230 ms) in low-performing sets; interaction F(1,3274)=4.23, p=.040. Overall pause means differed by 430 ms between performance groups (Cohen’s d=.99); pause variability d=1.05. These are observational associations, not evidence that inserting silence improves humor. The reported degrees of freedom and aggregation need clarification before treating these as independent paired observations. [Paper, methods and results](https://escholarship.org/content/qt58t1w6r7/qt58t1w6r7.pdf)

The [project page](https://mayuxi.com/research/talkshow/) labels links “Code” and “Dataset,” but they resolved back to the page in this check; the [author’s publication page](https://yzhu.io/publication/humor2026cogsci/) provides PDF and project links. No usable code/data release was verified. Reuse the published gap and within-performance quintile definitions where possible; exact embedding replication would require a paid model outside this study’s default scope.

**Pickering, Corduas et al. (2009), English humorous narratives.** The brief’s author-profile link is ambiguous; this is the relevant Corduas paper. The authors manually measured silent intervals using waveform selection and spectrogram cursors in CSL. They compared setup pauses with pauses around/within punchline intonational phrases in 20 performances, and separately examined substantial pre-punchline pauses (approximately 600–800 ms). Punchline-region pauses were 94 ms shorter, not significantly different (two-sided Wilcoxon p=.1447); 75% lacked a substantial preceding pause. No standardized pause effect or confidence interval is reported in that comparison. A nonsignificant result is not proof of equivalence. Their regional measure differs from our single boundary gap. [Author-hosted paper, procedures and Tables 4–5](https://faculty.tamuc.edu/lpickering/Pdfs/Publish_3.pdf)

**Attardo & Pickering (2011)** also report the −94 ms contrast and rounded means of 510 versus 420 ms. This related analysis must not be counted as an independent replication of the identical pause table. [Paper, §4.2 and Table 2](https://faculty.tamuc.edu/lpickering/Pdfs/Publish_11.pdf)

**Implication:** token-surprisal correlations are an extension, not a direct replication of Ma et al. Report a separate embedding-distance/quintile analysis, clearly labeled as an open-model approximation; do not compare a correlation numerically to a millisecond contrast.
